OBD360 is a vehicle diagnostic app that connects to an external OBD2 adapter over Bluetooth LE or
Wi-Fi. No account is required to launch the app or browse its core features.

**How to use the app without a car/adapter present:** the home screen shows two entry points, "OBD
scanner" and "VIN Check." VIN Check (vehicle history lookup by VIN) works fully without any
hardware — it only needs a VIN and an internet connection. The OBD scanner flow requires a
connected OBD2 adapter and a vehicle; without one, the Bluetooth screen shows a clear "adapter not
found" state rather than a blank or broken screen.

**Main features:** OBD2 trouble-code scan/clear, live sensor data, module-level diagnostics for
supported vehicles, AI-assisted explanation of results, fuel/service tracking, VIN history
lookup.

**Account / sign-in:** optional for browsing; required only to purchase or restore the PRO
subscription (so an entitlement can be tied to the account and sync across devices). Sign-in
methods: email/password, Google, or Sign in with Apple.

**In-app purchase:** from the PRO tab (or when opening a PRO-gated feature), reviewers can see
three subscription plans — OBD PRO, VIN PRO, and Full PRO — each with Monthly and Yearly billing,
under the "OBD360 PRO Plans" subscription group, plus one-time VIN-credit packs.

**Regions:** functionality is identical worldwide; pricing follows the App Store's own per-region
localization.
